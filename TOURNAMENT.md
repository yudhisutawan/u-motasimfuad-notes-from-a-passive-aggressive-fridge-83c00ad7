# Tournament — Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge

Judged head-to-head on the house criteria, in their published order:
(1) funny passive-aggressive note from the fridge; (2) real joke, one line,
turn/sting the reader does not see coming; (3) fridge voice — smug about
sensors, inventory, door alarms, petty about habits; (4) grudge small and
domestic; (5) fresh concrete detail, not generic nagging. First criterion
where two differ decides, unless a later one differs by much more.
No ties: every matchup names a winner and a margin (1 = by a hair,
5 = overwhelmingly better).
