## The entry

> Your leftovers are still here. They asked me to tell you they've moved on. I haven't.

One line, 85 characters — a note from a smart fridge to its owner, chosen by
a head-to-head tournament over 13 candidates (1 from Hermes, 12 built
locally) judged on the house criteria: from the fridge, a real joke with a
turn the reader does not see coming, fridge voice, small domestic grudge,
fresh concrete detail. The winner went 12–0. See
[CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) and [TOURNAMENT.md](TOURNAMENT.md).

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the winning line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. It never writes into the repository
itself. A length guard asserts the text stays exactly 85 characters.
